      • Why CineStill 800T at night: It's balanced for artificial light, so neon and lanterns look natural instead of orange, and bright signs get its signature red halo.
      • Shutter rule of thumb: Handheld, don't go slower than 1/your focal length (1/30 or faster on a 35mm lens) unless you're braced.
      • Where to buy film: Yodobashi Camera and Kitamura Camera in Shinjuku, both Monday stops, and Bic Camera as a backup. Stock varies, so if CineStill 800T is sold out, Portra 800 is the closest swap for night shots.
      • Developing plan: Wednesday at 10 AM, drop Monday's and Tuesday's rolls at Kitamura LABI Ikebukuro (scans to your phone that day). Negatives are usually ready by Friday: Friday is a free day and LABI is 1 stop from the hotel, so pick them up before your 5 PM car. Rolls from Wednesday afternoon on go home with you for your lab there. Backup: Yellow Jacket in Sendagi emails scans within a few hours (closed Wednesdays).
      • Film at the airport: At Narita and SFO security, ask for a hand check of film in your carry-on. Never put film in checked bags.

      Night shooting tips

      • Tripods: Leave the tripod behind. Bars like New York Bar won't allow them, and they're impractical in Akihabara, Golden Gai and Omoide Yokocho. Use a fast lens and push ISO.
      • Shooting through glass: Press a rubber lens hood flat against the window to kill reflections.
      • Rain: Wet streets double the neon, so a drizzly night is a good night to shoot.
      • People: In Golden Gai and Omoide Yokocho, shoot the lanes, not people's faces, and ask first.

      Before you fly

        Tax-free changed on Nov 1

        Pay full price now, get the 10% back at Narita. This applies to the camera, clothes and whisky.
        • In stores: Show your passport. You pay full price, tax included. The minimum is ¥5,000 before tax, per store, per day. Ask how they'll send the refund.
        • At Narita, 11/13: Before you check bags, scan your passport at a tax-free kiosk in the departure lobby. If it flags an inspection, show customs the items.
        • Keep everything unused and with you until then. You can't get anything back out of checked luggage.

        On the ground

        • Passport: Carry the physical passport every day. Japan requires it, and you need it for tax-free.
        • Cash: Bring yen for Golden Gai cover charges and Tsukiji stalls. 7-Eleven ATMs take US cards.
        • Cover charges: At yokocho and Golden Gai bars, ask about the charge before ordering.

        Coming home

        • US customs: $800 duty-free per person, including about 1 liter of alcohol. More whisky is fine, but you'll pay a small duty. Declare it all.
        • Whisky: Over 100 ml can't go in your carry-on, so pack it in checked bags with bottle sleeves.
        • Getting to Narita: Car pickup at OMO5 at 5:00 PM, about 60–90 min to Terminal 1 depending on traffic. You'll arrive around 6:00–6:30 PM, well ahead of the 9:25 PM flight.
